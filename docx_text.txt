2. Homepage Overview & Themes
International Conference on Multidisciplinary Research, Innovation, and Applications 2027 (ICMRIA 2027)
“Connecting Knowledge, Innovation and Society for a Sustainable and Intelligent Future”
The International Conference on Multidisciplinary Research and Innovation 2027 (ICMRI 2027) is an international forum, sponsored by Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh, that provides a platform for the international academic community, researchers, industry practitioners, policymakers and students from all over the world. The conference provides a common forum for exchanging ideas, original research, and networking with colleagues from other disciplines to inform a sustainable and intelligent future.
Given ICMRI 2027's focus on bringing together the best minds to address today's most pressing challenges, the conference welcomes contributions that connect knowledge, innovation, and society across technology, environment, business, health, humanities, and governance.

5. Author Guidelines, Registration & Costs
Author Guidelines
Authors are invited to submit original and unpublished research abstracts related to the conference theme and tracks. All submissions will undergo peer review. Submission guidelines:
Abstracts should be 200–250 words, clearly stating the objective, methodology, findings, and relevance to the conference theme.
Include paper title, author name(s), affiliation(s), country, corresponding author's email, selected track, and 4–6 keywords.
Abstracts may be submitted in DOCX or PDF format through the conference submission portal/email.
Full manuscripts must follow the conference's prescribed template and formatting guidelines (to be published on the website).
All submissions must be original, unpublished, and not under review elsewhere.
Each accepted abstract must be presented (onsite or online) by at least one registered author.
Submission Portal: All papers must be submitted electronically through the conference submission system.
Formatting & Template: Papers must adhere strictly to standard IEEE conference paper formatting guidelines.
Camera-Ready Requirements: Authors of accepted papers must incorporate reviewers' comments and submit the final camera-ready version along with the completed copyright transfer form.
Registration Info & Cost Structure
Registration details apply to regular authors, student authors, key participants, and international delegates.
At least one author of each accepted paper must register at the full rate to ensure inclusion in the conference program and proceedings.

Submission Portal Management Instruction for DIU Web Team
System Architecture & Workflow Overview
[Author] ──(Submits Abstract/Paper)──► [System Portal]
                                            │
[Admin / TPC Chair] ──(Assigns Papers)──────┼──► [Reviewer / TPC Member]
                                            │           │
[Author] ◄──(Receives Acceptance/Feedback)──┴──(Submits Evaluation)
Phase 1: Pre-Submission & Account Setup
User Registration & Profile Creation
Users create an account using their primary email address, full name, affiliation (e.g., Daffodil International University), country, and ORCID ID.
System assigns roles dynamically (Author, Reviewer, Track Chair, TPC Chair, Admin). A single user can have multiple roles.
Conference & Track Configuration (Admin Side)
Track Setup: Admin creates conference tracks and sub-tracks (e.g., Track 1: AI & Data Science).
Submission Rules: Define paper limits, submission deadlines, file formats (.pdf, .docx), and blind review settings (Single-Blind or Double-Blind).
Custom Forms: Add required fields like Paper Title, Abstract (200–250 words), Keywords, and Topic Categories.
Phase 2: Paper Submission Process (Author Side)
New Submission Initialization
The author logs in and clicks "Create New Submission".
Selects the target Track and Sub-Track.
Metadata Entry
Enter the Paper Title, Abstract, and 4–6 Keywords.
Adds all Co-Authors with their names, emails, and affiliations, and selects the Corresponding Author.
Conflict of Interest (CoI) Declaration
Authors check off any potential Conflicts of Interest with institutional reviewers/chairs to ensure unbiased review assignments.
File Upload & Confirmation
Author uploads the anonymized manuscript file following the conference template (e.g., IEEE format).
The system generates a unique Paper ID (e.g., ICMRI2027-104) and sends an automated confirmation email to all co-authors.
Authors can edit or replace the submitted draft anytime before the submission deadline.
Phase 3: Review Assignment & Bidding (Chair & Reviewer Side)
Paper Bidding (Optional)
System presents reviewers with paper titles and abstracts. Reviewers mark their preference: Want to Review, Can Review, Neutral, or Conflict.
Reviewer Assignment
Automatic Assignment: System matches reviewers to papers based on subject keywords, reviewer expertise, workload limits, and conflict detection.
Manual Assignment: Track Chairs manually assign at least 2 to 3 independent reviewers per paper.
Phase 4: Reviewing & Evaluation Process (Reviewer Side)
Reviewer Dashboard
Reviewer accesses assigned manuscripts via their reviewer panel and downloads anonymized PDF/DOCX files.
Evaluation Form Submission
Reviewers fill out a standardized evaluation form including:
Originality & Novelty (Score: 1 to 5)
Technical Soundness & Methodology (Score: 1 to 5)
Relevance to Conference Theme (Score: 1 to 5)
Overall Score / Recommendation: Strong Accept, Accept, Borderline, Reject, or Strong Reject.
Feedback for Authors: Constructive comments detailing required improvements.
Confidential Comments for Track Chairs: Internal notes visible only to committee leaders.
Phase 5: Decision Making & Notification (Track & TPC Chair Side)
Review Consolidation & Discussion
Track Chairs review evaluation reports for each paper. If scores conflict (e.g., one Accept and one Reject), a third reviewer is assigned or an internal discussion is opened.
Final Decision Entry
Track Chair submits a formal decision recommendation: Accept, Accept with Minor Revisions, or Reject.
TPC Chair grants final approval for all track decisions.
Automated Notification Email
System sends bulk decision notifications to corresponding authors containing reviewer feedback (without revealing reviewer identity).
Phase 6: Camera-Ready Submission & Registration
Camera-Ready Upload
Authors of accepted papers update their manuscript according to reviewer comments, add author names/affiliations, and upload the final camera-ready version.
Authors upload the signed Copyright Transfer Form.
Registration & Fee Verification
Author inputs transaction details or uploads payment proof for conference registration.
Admin verifies payment and marks the paper as "Confirmed for Proceedings".
Program Scheduling & Export
System exports accepted paper data, author details, and session schedules into XML/JSON or PDF format to auto-generate the conference schedule, book of abstracts, and proceedings.
