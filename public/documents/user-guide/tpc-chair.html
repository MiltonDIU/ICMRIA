TPC Chair Guide

Technical Programme Committee Chair — ICMRIA 2027 submission portal

In short: the track chairs recommend a decision for each paper; you approve it or return it, then release the decisions to authors with Send decision emails.

What you see

You can read every paper, its evaluations and its decision history in every track (menu Decisions). You do not assign reviewers, review papers or enter decisions yourself (unless the organisers enable "change decision" below).

1. Final Approval

Menu Final Approval, three tabs:

TabWhat you do
Awaiting approvalTick decisions and press Approve the ticked decisions, or Return one to its chair with what to reconsider.
Returned to chairsDecisions waiting for the chair to revise and send again.
ApprovedApproved decisions and whether the authors have been emailed.
Approving does not email anyone. Authors are told only when you press Send decision emails, so you can catch a mistake and release all tracks' results together.

2. Sending the decisions

  • Send decision emails emails each approved, not-yet-notified paper: the decision, the chair's note and the reviewers' scores and feedback (never reviewer names or confidential comments).
  • If an email never arrived (for example a mail server problem), use Resend email on that paper in the Approved tab.

3. Comments

Use Comments between the chairs and the TPC Chair on a paper's decision page to discuss a decision before approving it. Every round's comments are kept.

4. Changing a decision yourself (only if enabled)

If the organisers turn this on, a Change decision button appears on papers awaiting approval or returned. Choose Accept, Accept with Minor Revisions or Reject, give the reason, and press Set & approve. The change and reason are recorded in the paper's history. Otherwise, to reject a paper the chair recommended accepting, Return it with your reason.

Conflicts

A paper you wrote, or one whose author declared a conflict with you, is skipped when you approve in bulk; another member of the committee handles it.
