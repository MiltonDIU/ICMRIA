Administrator Guide

SuperAdmin and Admin — ICMRIA 2027 submission portal

Administrators see every track and can do what chairs and the TPC Chair do, plus run the conference: users and roles, settings, proceedings (confirmation, programme, exports) and the history of every change. Read the other guides too — they describe what each role sees.

1. The paper's path

abstract → full manuscript → reviewers assigned → evaluations → chair's decision → TPC approval → decision email → (revision checked by chair) → camera-ready → copyright form → attendance & payment → Confirm (admin) → programme session. Every step is recorded with who did it and when.

The Papers list shows each paper's stage, a Manuscript column and filters. The old abstract screening (approve / reject) is optional: rejecting there acts as a desk reject (the paper is never reviewed).

2. Users and roles

RoleCan
AuthorSubmit and follow their papers, pay. (Participants share this role but see no paper pages.)
ReviewerBid, accept/decline and evaluate assigned papers.
Track Chair / Sub-Track ChairReviewer pool, assignment, decisions, revision checks in their scope.
TPC ChairFinal approval and sending decision emails; reads everything.
Admin / SuperAdminEverything, conference-wide.
A person can hold several roles. When adding a role under Users, keep the existing ones ticked — saving replaces the whole list (e.g. a chair given the Reviewer role must keep the Sub-Track Chair role too). Chairs are attached to their track under Tracks & Chairs; the role alone is not enough.

3. Settings

Menu Settings. Every row now shows what it does and the accepted values; the edit page repeats it. Dates are entered as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS; switches as true / false. The ones that change the workflow:

SettingDefaultEffect
review_requires_manuscripttrueReviewers only for papers with a manuscript.
review_waits_for_manuscript_deadlinetrueNo assignment before the manuscript deadline.
manuscript_locks_on_reviewtrueAuthor cannot replace the manuscript once a reviewer has it.
chairs_can_review_own_trackfalseChairs may review in their own track.
reviewer_chair_can_decidefalseA chair who reviewed a paper may still decide it.
tpc_can_override_decisionfalseTPC Chair may set the decision directly.
min_reviewers_per_paper2Evaluations needed before a decision.
review_feedback_min_chars0Shortest feedback for authors.
email_all_authorsfalsePaper emails to all authors (true) or the corresponding author.
attendance_change_enabledtrueAuthors may change who attends until paying.
manual_payment_enabledfalseAllow bank/mobile transfer reports for verification.
author_conflict_declaration_enabledtrueAuthors may declare conflicts.
is_payment_enabled, payment_last_date, max_paid_papers_limitWhen payment is open.
early_registration_start_date / _last_dateEarly-bird window.

4. Proceedings

Menu Proceedings:

  • Camera-ready tab: each accepted paper's camera-ready, copyright and fee. When all are in, press Confirm (online payments do not confirm automatically). Use Request changes with a note to reopen the author's upload.
  • Payments to verify: only used when manual transfers are enabled.
  • Confirmed for Proceedings: place each paper in a programme session and slot.
  • Upload the blank Copyright Transfer Form here before camera-ready opens.
  • Exports: JSON, XML, Book of Abstracts (PDF), Programme (PDF), camera-ready files (ZIP), and Progress history (CSV).

5. Reviewers' expertise

On a reviewer's profile (Reviewers by Track → click the name) administrators can edit the whole expertise list, removing as well as adding keywords. It applies to every track the reviewer serves.

7. Email

Emails are sent by a queue worker. Keep php artisan queue:work running on the server (Supervisor or cron). After any change to .env (for example the mail settings) run php artisan queue:restart, or the worker keeps the old settings and emails fail. Failed emails are listed in the failed_jobs table; a decision email can be re-sent from Final Approval → Approved → Resend email.

8. After deploying an update

git pull → php artisan migrate --force → php artisan optimize:clear → php artisan queue:restart.
