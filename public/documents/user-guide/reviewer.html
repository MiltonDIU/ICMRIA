Reviewer Guide

ICMRIA 2027 submission portal

In short: record your research keywords → bid on papers (if open) → accept or decline each paper you are given → read the manuscript → save and submit your evaluation → join the discussion if the chair opens one.

1. Your account and keywords

  • You receive an account from the conference team; set your password with the reset link.
  • Open My Reviews and fill in Research Areas / Keywords (1–20). Pick existing terms where you can — papers are matched to reviewers by exact keywords, so "Machine Learning" matches a paper's "machine learning", but "ML" does not.
  • The chairs may also record your expertise for their track; both lists are used.

2. Bidding (if open)

When bidding is open, the Paper Bidding page lists papers by title and abstract. Mark each one:

ChoiceMeaning
Want to ReviewYou would like this paper; it raises your chance of getting it.
Can ReviewYou are able to review it.
NeutralNo preference.
ConflictYou must not review it (you know the authors, it is your own work, etc.). You will never be given it.

3. Receiving a paper

  • When a chair assigns you a paper you receive an email. Papers are assigned only after the author's full manuscript is in (and, by default, after the manuscript deadline), so the file you read is final.
  • Open the paper under My Reviews and accept it, or decline it with a short reason — the chair is told at once so someone else can take it.
  • Download the manuscript. Review is double-blind: author names are not shown, and the file is named by its paper ID. If you recognise the work and cannot judge it impartially, decline.

4. Writing your evaluation

PartWhat to giveWho sees it
Originality & NoveltyScore 1–5 (Poor … Excellent)Chairs; authors see scores without your name
Technical Soundness & MethodologyScore 1–5Same
Relevance to Conference ThemeScore 1–5Same
Overall recommendationStrong Accept, Accept, Borderline, Reject, Strong RejectChairs only
Feedback for authorsRequired. A short comment is enough; where you can, say what works and what to improve.Authors, with the decision, without your name
Confidential commentsOptional notes for the chairsChairs and TPC Chair only
  • Save draft as often as you like. Submit when finished.
  • You cannot submit until the author's manuscript is on file.
A submitted evaluation can no longer be changed.

5. Discussion

If reviewers disagree, the chair may open an internal discussion. You can join it once you have submitted your own evaluation, so nothing you read there can shape it. Reviewers appear to each other as "Reviewer 1", "Reviewer 2"; the chairs see names. Authors never see the discussion.

6. Workload

Each reviewer holds a limited number of open papers at once (set by the organisers). Chairs see your load before giving you more.

If you are also a chair: by default you do not review papers in a track you chair. The organisers can allow it; if you then review a paper, another chair decides on it (unless the organisers allow otherwise).
