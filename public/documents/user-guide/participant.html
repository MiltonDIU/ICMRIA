Participant Guide

For those attending ICMRIA 2027 without presenting a paper

In short: register as a participant → pay the registration fee online → receive your registration ID.

1. Registering

  1. Open the registration page on the conference website and choose to register as a participant.
  2. Fill in your details and choose your delegate category (Student, Academic or Industry/R&D for Bangladesh; SAARC or International otherwise). Only the categories your country qualifies for are offered. The category sets your fee.
  3. Verify your email address from the message you receive.

2. Paying

  • Sign in and open My Profile (or your dashboard). The Pay your registration fee card shows the amount due.
  • The amount follows today's rate: early-bird during the early-bird window, regular after it. It is recalculated each time you open the page and again at checkout, so you are always charged the rate in force on the day you pay.
  • Press Review & Pay and complete the payment through OneCard (cards and mobile payments; foreign payments are converted from USD at the day's rate).
  • When the payment succeeds you receive a confirmation email and your registration ID.
Once paid, your registration is settled: the amount paid never changes, and the portal will not take a second payment. Payment closes at the payment deadline, or earlier if the organisers close it.

3. What you will not see

As a participant you have no papers, so the Papers pages and the "submit an abstract" options are not shown to you. If you later decide to present a paper, contact the conference team.
