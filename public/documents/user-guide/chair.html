Track Chair & Sub-Track Chair Guide

ICMRIA 2027 submission portal

In short: build your track's reviewer pool → assign reviewers to papers → once enough evaluations are in, enter a decision (it goes to the TPC Chair for approval) → check revised manuscripts of papers accepted with minor revisions.

Your scope

  • A Track Chair works on the whole track, including every sub-track under it.
  • A Sub-Track Chair (co-chair) works on their own sub-track only.
  • You never handle a paper you wrote, or one whose author declared a conflict with you — another chair does.

1. Reviewers by Track

Menu Reviewers by Track. Each track or sub-track you chair has a card:

  • Add someone who already reviews: search by name, email or expertise (the top 10 matches show first; type to find anyone). A ★ marks people whose expertise overlaps the subject.
  • Or invite somebody new: name and email. An existing account with that email is given the Reviewer role and added; a new email creates an account and the person sets their password from the reset link.
  • Expertise here: pick existing keywords or type new ones. Keywords drive automatic assignment, so use the terms authors use.
  • Click a reviewer's name to see their profile, expertise, the tracks they serve and the papers they hold.

2. Assigning reviewers

Menu Assign Reviewers. Open a paper, or use Auto-assign every paper that needs reviewers.

  • Automatic assignment ranks reviewers by bids ("Want/Can review"), then by how many of the paper's keywords match their expertise.
  • The system refuses, with a reason shown next to the name: authors of the paper, anyone with a declared or bid conflict, anyone over their paper limit, people outside the track's pool, and the paper's own chairs.
  • By default a paper can receive reviewers only after its full manuscript is uploaded and after the manuscript deadline, so every reviewer reads the final file. (Organisers can change both.)
  • Once a reviewer has the paper, the author can no longer replace the manuscript.
  • If a reviewer declines you are emailed; assign someone in their place.

3. Decisions

Menu Decisions. Each paper shows its evaluations, average scores and the reviewers' recommendations.

  1. The decision form opens once the minimum number of evaluations are submitted (shown at the top of the form while locked).
  2. Choose Accept, Accept with Minor Revisions or Reject.
  3. Note to the authors (optional) goes to the authors with the reviewers' feedback.
  4. Your comment as chair (optional) is kept with this round for the TPC Chair.
  5. Press Send for TPC approval. The TPC Chair approves it, or returns it with a reason — then revise and send again (a new round). Authors are emailed only after approval, when the decision emails are sent.

4. Revised manuscripts

Menu Revised Manuscripts. For papers accepted with minor revisions, download the revision, read the author's summary of changes, and Approve revision or Send back with a note. The author's camera-ready step opens only after you approve.

5. Reviewing in your own track (only if enabled)

By default you do not review papers in a track you chair. The organisers can allow it when reviewers are short. If you then review a paper, another chair enters its decision — unless the organisers also allow a reviewing chair to decide.
