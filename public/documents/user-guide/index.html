ICMRIA 2027 — Portal User Guides

International Conference on Multidisciplinary Research, Innovation and Applications · Daffodil International University

Each guide explains what one role can do in the submission portal, in the order the work happens, and what is locked at each step. Pick the guide for your role.

AuthorSubmit an abstract and manuscript, follow the review, camera-ready, attendance and payment. ParticipantRegister to attend without a paper, and pay the registration fee. ReviewerKeywords, bidding, accepting papers, writing and submitting evaluations, discussion. Track Chair & Sub-Track ChairReviewer pool, assigning reviewers, decisions, revised manuscripts, camera-ready check. TPC ChairFinal approval of decisions and sending the decision emails. AdministratorUsers and roles, settings, proceedings, payments, programme and exports.
Some rules described here can be changed by the organisers in the portal's Settings (for example dates, fees, or whether a step is open). Where a rule depends on a setting, the guide says so. If the portal shows something different from this guide, the portal reflects the organisers' current choice.
