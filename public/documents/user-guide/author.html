Author Guide

ICMRIA 2027 submission portal

In short: submit your abstract → upload the full manuscript → your paper is reviewed → you receive the decision by email → if accepted, upload the camera-ready manuscript and the signed copyright form, confirm who will attend → pay the registration fee → the conference team confirms your paper for the proceedings.

1. Submitting your abstract

  1. Register on the conference website as an author, or sign in and open Papers → Submit New Abstract.
  2. Enter the title, the abstract (200–250 words), 4–6 keywords, and choose the track and sub-track.
  3. Add every author with name, email, designation, institution and country, and choose each author's delegate category (Student, Academic, Industry/R&D, SAARC or International). The category sets that author's fee; choose Student for a student author. Only the categories an author's country qualifies for are offered.
  4. Mark the corresponding author (who receives the emails) and the presenting author.
  5. Tick "This author will attend the conference" for each author who will come. The fee is charged only for attending authors; at least one must attend.
  6. If the organisers allow it, name any chair or reviewer of your track you have a conflict of interest with (supervisor, close collaborator, colleague). They will not review or decide on your paper.

Each account may submit a limited number of abstracts (set by the organisers). Keyword wording matters: reviewers are matched to your paper by keywords, so use common, specific terms (e.g. "Machine Learning", not "ML").

2. Editing your submission

You can edit the title, abstract, keywords, track and authors only while the abstract is still pending, the abstract deadline has not passed, and no reviewer has been given the paper. After that the submission is fixed.

3. Uploading the full manuscript

  • Upload it from your paper's page during the manuscript window (see Important Dates).
  • PDF or Word, up to 20 MB, in the IEEE conference template, within the page limit (6–8 pages).
  • Review is double-blind: remove all author names and affiliations from the file and confirm it on upload.
  • You may replace the file until the window closes or a reviewer is assigned, whichever comes first. Every earlier version is kept on record.

4. Review

Your paper's stage shows "Under review" while reviewers evaluate it. You will not see internal steps (the chair's recommendation, the TPC Chair's approval) until the decision email is sent.

5. The decision

You receive an email with the decision and the reviewers' feedback (reviewer names are never shown):

DecisionWhat happens next
AcceptGo to step 7 (camera-ready).
Accept with Minor RevisionsGo to step 6 first.
RejectNothing further; no fee is due.

6. Minor revisions (if asked)

  1. On your paper's page, upload the revised manuscript and summarise how you addressed each reviewer comment, before the revision deadline.
  2. The track chair checks it and either approves it or sends it back with a note (you then upload again).
  3. The camera-ready step opens only once the revision is approved.

7. Camera-ready manuscript and copyright form

  1. Upload the camera-ready manuscript — the final version, now with every author name and affiliation, following the template.
  2. Then download the blank Copyright Transfer Form from your paper's page, sign it, and upload it.
  3. Both are due by the camera-ready deadline. The copyright form can be uploaded only after the camera-ready manuscript.

8. Who will attend

On your paper's page, Who Will Attend lists every author. Tick those who will come and press Save attendance. The fee is the sum of each ticked author's fee at their own delegate category. You can change this right up to the moment you pay (unless the organisers have closed changes). Saving it also confirms your author list, which payment requires.

9. Paying the registration fee

  • Payment opens only after the camera-ready manuscript and the copyright form are in.
  • Pay with the Pay … now button on your paper's page, or the Pay button in your Papers list. The breakdown shows each attending author, their category and amount.
  • Payment is online through OneCard (cards and mobile payments; foreign payments are converted from USD at the day's rate).
  • Early-bird or regular rates apply by the payment date, recalculated when you pay. Once paid, the amount never changes.
  • You receive a confirmation email and your registration ID.

10. After payment

Your part is done and your files are final. The conference team checks them and marks your paper Confirmed for Proceedings; your presentation session then appears on your paper's page. If the team asks for changes to the camera-ready files, the upload reopens until you send corrected files.

Your paper's stage

The Papers list, your paper's page and your dashboard show one stage, for example "Manuscript submitted", "Under review", "Accepted — camera-ready due", "Accepted — copyright form due", "Accepted — registration fee due", "Accepted — paid, awaiting confirmation" or "Confirmed for Proceedings". It always tells you what is outstanding.

Emails go to the corresponding author (or to every author, if the organisers have chosen that). Check your Spam folder if a message does not arrive, and keep your profile's email address current.
